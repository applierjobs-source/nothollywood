Common questions

How does the credit system work?

Buy a pack once, credits go into your account, credits never expire. When you render a video, credits are deducted based on the finished length: 1 credit per second at 768P, 2 credits per second at 2K. You see the credit cost before you confirm each render.

What if a render fails?

Failed renders are refunded automatically. Credits go straight back into your balance. You never pay for something that didn't work.

Can I get a refund on unused credits?

Yes. Email zach@nothollywood.ai within 30 days of purchase and we'll refund your unused credit balance, prorated. Used credits are non-refundable.

What resolution do I get?

Every render is native 768P from MiniMax H3 with built-in synchronized audio and voice. 2K is available on any pack at 2× credit cost per second. Native audio, sound effects, and lip-synced dialogue are included at no extra credit cost — the model generates everything in one pass.

Do you store my videos?

Videos are held in memory only during your active session. Download them right after they render or they may disappear when we redeploy. Deliberate choice — cheaper, faster, less of your content on our servers.

Is there really no content filter?

Correct. We run MiniMax H3 with content_filter set to false. You're responsible for what you make. The model itself refuses to generate CSAM or non-consensual likenesses of real people. Everything else is on you.
