Common questions

What counts as a "minute" of render time?

The total duration of finished video you export. A 6-second scene counts as 6 seconds. A 45-second stitched multi-scene show counts as 45 seconds. Failed renders don't count.

Is there really no content filter?

Correct. We run our video model with content_filter set to false. You're responsible for what you make. We don't generate CSAM or content depicting real people without consent — the model refuses those. Everything else is on you.

Can I remove the watermark on the Pro plan?

No — watermark removal is a Studio-tier feature and is part of the commercial license. It's how we track which clips are attributable to paying commercial users.

What happens if I go over my included minutes?

You get charged the overage rate ($4/min on Pro, $3/min on Studio) at the end of the billing cycle. No hard stop — your renders keep going. You can set an overage cap in your account settings.

Do you store my videos?

Videos are held in Railway memory only while your session is active. Download to keep them. This is a deliberate choice — cheaper, faster, and less of your content on our servers.

What about refunds?

Prorated refund within 7 days if you haven't used more than 10% of your monthly minute allowance. Email zach@nothollywood.ai.
